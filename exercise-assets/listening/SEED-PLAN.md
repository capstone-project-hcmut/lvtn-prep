Wizard notes:
- Template types (form / note / sentence / summary / flow chart): type the blank as exactly `___` (three underscores). Only the first `___` in a line becomes the blank; blanks are auto-numbered, so do not type "(6)".
- Grading is exact match after trim + lowercase + whitespace collapse. That is why number answers list every spelling.
- Table blanks have no explanation field in the wizard. Their explanations below are for reference only.
- Transcripts are in `L*-transcript.txt`. The wizard has no transcript field (L1 has `transcript: null` as well).

## L2 — Joining Riverside

**Name:** Joining Riverside

**Description:**
A phone call to a leisure centre from someone who wants to become a member. Almost everything you need is a name, a number or a date, and some of them are spelled out letter by letter. Listen for the detail that is actually chosen, not every option the receptionist offers. A form, a table of classes, then three short answers.

**Duration:** 600 s
**Competencies:** `list_spec_info` 0.7 · `list_speech_rec` 0.3
**Audio:** `L2-riverside-centre.mp3`

### Group 1 — Form Completion (Q1–6)
**Instructions:** Complete the form below. Write ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.

| # | Line (type as question text) | Accepted | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `Surname: ___` | `Hartley` | Daniel spells it: "H-A-R-T-L-E-Y", and Alison reads it back. The spelling is the test, because "Hartly" or "Heartley" are both plausible from the sound alone. |
| 2 | `Address: 22 ___ Road` | `Beckford` | "Twenty-two Beckford Road. Beckford is B-E-C-K-F-O-R-D." The house number and "Road" are given on the form, so only the spelled name is missing. |
| 3 | `Phone number: ___` | `0411623895` · `0411 623 895` · `0411-623-895` | "Oh four double one, six two three, eight nine five." Two conventions to decode: "oh" is zero and "double one" is 11. Alison's read-back ("oh four one one") confirms it. |
| 4 | `Membership type: ___` | `Premium` | Three types are offered. Off-peak is ruled out ("I don't finish work until five, so Off-peak's no good") and Standard is never chosen: "I'll take Premium." |
| 5 | `Start date: 1 ___` | `March` | "The first of March, if that's possible." The day is already on the form. |
| 6 | `Heard about the centre from: a ___` | `colleague` | "A colleague recommended it, actually." |

### Group 2 — Table Completion (Q7–10)
**Instructions:** Complete the table below. Write ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.
**Table title:** Classes

Grid, 4 rows × 5 columns (row 1 is a plain-text header row, `[Q]` = convert the cell to a blank):

| Class | Day | Time (pm / am) | Length (minutes) | Extra cost ($ per session) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aqua Fitness | Monday | 7 pm | 60 | `[Q7]` |
| Yoga | `[Q8]` | 6.30 pm | `[Q9]` | none |
| Circuit Training | Saturday | `[Q10]` | 60 | none |

Table cells are either text or a blank, so they can't mix the two. That's why units sit in the headers and the Q10 cell is only the number. Q10's accepted answers include `9 am` so a student who adds the unit isn't marked wrong.

| # | Accepted | Explanation (reference only) |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | `4` · `four` | "Aqua Fitness has a small extra charge, four dollars a session." Yoga and Circuit Training are "included in Premium", so they have no extra cost. |
| 8 | `Wednesday` · `Wednesdays` | "Yoga is Wednesdays at six thirty." |
| 9 | `75` · `seventy-five` · `seventy five` | "that one's seventy-five minutes." The other two classes are "an hour", so don't carry 60 across. |
| 10 | `9` · `nine` · `9 am` · `9am` | "Circuit Training is Saturday mornings at nine." |

### Group 3 — Short Answer (Q11–13)
**Instructions:** Answer the questions below. Write NO MORE THAN TWO WORDS AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.
**Constraints:** max words 2 · max numbers 1

| # | Question | Accepted | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Why is there an extra charge for Aqua Fitness? | `outside instructor` · `an outside instructor` | "because we bring in an outside instructor." "an" is accepted because the two-word limit counts it separately and some learners include it. |
| 12 | What must Daniel bring on his first visit? | `photo identification` · `photo ID` · `photographic identification` | "Just photo identification. A driving licence or a passport is fine." The licence and passport are examples of it, not the answer. |
| 13 | For how long can members park free of charge? | `two hours` · `2 hours` · `2 hrs` | "It's free for the first two hours for members." |

## L3 — Volunteer Day at Kestrel Point

**Name:** Volunteer Day at Kestrel Point

**Description:**
A warden briefs new volunteers at a coastal nature reserve. The first half is a walk around the map, so keep track of where the speaker is as they move. Several places are pointed out only to tell you they are *not* the one you want. The second half assigns the week's jobs and some practical details, and plans have changed since the timetable was printed. Five map labels, four matches from a box, four notes.

**Duration:** 600 s
**Competencies:** `list_spec_info` 0.6 · `list_predict` 0.4
**Audio:** `L3-kestrel-point.mp3`

### Group 1 — Map Labelling (Q1–5)
**Instructions:** Label the map below. Write the correct letter, A–H, next to Questions 1–5.
**Image:** `L3-map.png`
**Map labels pool:** A `A` · B `B` · C `C` · D `D` · E `E` · F `F` · G `G` · H `H`
(The pool text is just the letter, because the positions are on the map. Use `Point A` etc. if the UI looks bare.)

| # | Location | Answer | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tool store | B | "The tool store is the green shed directly opposite us, on the other side of the main path." A is the trap: the "little brick building on the edge of the car park… That's not it. That's the electricity supply." |
| 2 | Seed nursery | C | "About a third of the way along, there's a smaller path going off to the left, towards the woods. Follow that to the very end, where the trees start." |
| 3 | First-aid point | H | "you come to a second junction, where a path branches off to the right, towards the sea. The first-aid point is right there, at that junction, on the corner." |
| 4 | Bird hide | E | The speaker sets up D and then rejects it: benches and a jetty on the south shore, "a lot of visitors assume that's where the bird hide is. It isn't. The hide is on the opposite side, on the far, northern shore." |
| 5 | Meeting point for the beach clean | F | "We don't meet on the beach itself… The meeting point is the bridge." G, at the end of the path in the dunes, is where you'd expect a beach clean to meet, which is exactly why it's ruled out. |

### Group 2 — List of Options (Q6–9)
**Instructions:** What is the main task on each day? Choose FOUR answers from the box and write the correct letter, A–G, next to Questions 6–9.
**Options pool:**
- A clearing litter
- B cutting back scrub
- C counting birds
- D planting trees
- E repairing fences
- F collecting seeds
- G building nest boxes

| # | Question | Answer | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Monday | E | "On Mondays, we'd originally planned to do the bird count, but… the count has moved to Saturday, and Monday is now fence repairs." C is heard first, but the plan changes. |
| 7 | Tuesday | B | "Tuesdays are for scrub. That means cutting back the bushes." D is mentioned straight afterwards only to be excluded: "And no tree planting, before anyone asks." |
| 8 | Thursday | F | "Thursdays used to be our litter day, but the local schools have taken that on… on Thursdays you'll be with me in the meadow, collecting seed." A is the old arrangement. |
| 9 | Saturday | C | "as I said, Saturday is the bird count." This confirms the change made in the Monday turn. G is never scheduled: "that won't be until next winter at the earliest." |

### Group 3 — Note Completion (Q10–13)
**Instructions:** Complete the notes below. Write ONE WORD AND/OR A NUMBER for each answer.

| # | Line | Accepted | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | `Wear strong boots and bring a ___ jacket` | `waterproof` | "Please wear strong boots, and bring a waterproof jacket." The note already contains "jacket", so the gap is the adjective. |
| 11 | `Hot drinks provided; bring your own ___` | `mug` | "we've stopped using disposable cups, so bring your own mug." "cup" is the word you hear first, but the speaker says cups are no longer used. |
| 12 | `Sign the ___ at the visitor centre desk on arrival and departure` | `register` | "When you arrive, and again when you leave, sign the register at the visitor centre desk." |
| 13 | `Minibus to the station leaves at ___ pm` | `4.15` · `4:15` · `quarter past four` | "The old timetable says four o'clock, but it now leaves at quarter past four." Writing 4 is the trap. |

## L4 — Redesigning the Study Hub

**Name:** Redesigning the Study Hub

**Description:**
Two students finalise a proposal to redesign their department's study space. Very little is decided in a single sentence. An idea is suggested, questioned and then changed, so wait for the point where they agree. In the second half, Nadia rarely states her opinion outright, so you'll need to work out what she thinks from how she reacts. Five labels on a floor plan, five opinions to match, and one question with two answers.

**Duration:** 600 s
**Competencies:** `list_infer` 0.6 · `list_gist` 0.2 · `list_spec_info` 0.2
**Audio:** `L4-study-hub.mp3`

### Group 1 — Plan Labelling (Q1–5)
**Instructions:** Label the plan below. Write the correct letter, A–G, next to Questions 1–5.
**Image:** `L4-plan.png`
**Plan labels pool:** A `A` · B `B` · C `C` · D `D` · E `E` · F `F` · G `G`

| # | Location | Answer | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quiet room | E | The original plan was F ("the corner by the windows, on the right-hand side"), but it's next to the lecture-theatre door. They move it: "the north-west corner. It's the furthest point from both doors, and it still gets the daylight." |
| 2 | Group booths | C | Oscar wants the middle (D), but Nadia says it would "block the view across the whole space" and suggests "along the left-hand wall, next to the stairs". Oscar agrees: "booths by the stairs." |
| 3 | Printers | G | "By the lecture theatre door, surely." "Agreed, that's the obvious place." |
| 4 | Café counter | B | Oscar assumes the left side, but "the only water supply on the ground floor is on the right-hand side of the entrance. So the café has to go there." |
| 5 | Lockers | A | "the café's to the right of the entrance, which leaves the space on the left for the lockers." |

### Group 2 — Matching (Q6–10)
**Instructions:** What is Nadia's opinion of each proposed change? Choose FIVE answers from the box and write the correct letter, A–F, next to Questions 6–10.
**Options pool:**
- A It would be too expensive.
- B It is unlikely to be used.
- C It should be tried out first.
- D It is the most important change.
- E It could make the room noisier.
- F It has already been approved.

| # | Item | Answer | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | a booking system for the booths | C | "Why not run it for a month and see what happens, before the department pays for a whole system?" B is Oscar's view ("I don't think anyone will bother to book"), and Nadia disagrees with it. The question asks what *Nadia* thinks. |
| 7 | moveable furniture | E | "the moment tables start moving, people start chatting, and then the quiet end of the room isn't quiet any more." She never says "noise"; you have to infer it from what she describes. |
| 8 | more power sockets | D | "if the committee only approves one thing on the list, it should be that." |
| 9 | a green wall | A | "it's not the plants, it's the maintenance contract… that alone would use up most of the budget." She likes the idea ("So do I, in principle"), so an answer based on her attitude to the plants would be wrong. |
| 10 | longer opening hours | F | "The faculty signed it off last week." "Signed off" means approved, so the option paraphrases it. |

### Group 3 — Multiple Choice, multi-answer (Q11–12)
**Instructions:** Choose TWO letters, A–E.
Set the question's answer mode to **multi**, with **required count 2**.

**Question:** Which TWO things do the students decide to include in their proposal to the committee?
- A cost estimates for each change
- B the results of the student survey
- C photos of study spaces at other universities
- D the revised floor plan
- E a timeline for the building work

**Accepted:** `B`, `D`

**Explanation:** Three suggestions are dropped, one after another. Costs are the estates office's job ("If we guess, they'll just correct us"). The photos would invite the reply "we're not them, and we don't have their money", and Oscar concedes: "Leave them out." A timeline "depends on when the builders are free". What survives is the survey ("That's our strongest argument") and the plan ("has to go in, obviously"). Oscar sums up both at the end: "the survey results, and the plan."

## L5 — Water Under the Desert

**Name:** Water Under the Desert

**Description:**
Part of a lecture on the qanat, a system of underground tunnels that has carried water across dry land for about three thousand years. The lecture follows the diagram first, then the order of construction, then what happened to qanats in the last century. Read ahead so you know what kind of word each gap needs, because the lecturer won't pause for you. Four diagram labels, a five-step flow chart and three sentences.

**Duration:** 600 s
**Competencies:** `list_predict` 0.4 · `list_spec_info` 0.4 · `list_speech_rec` 0.2
**Audio:** `L5-qanat.mp3`

### Group 1 — Diagram Labelling (Q1–4)
**Instructions:** Label the diagram below. Write ONE WORD ONLY for each answer.
**Image:** `L5-diagram.png` (the numbers 1–4 are printed on the image)
**Constraints:** max words 1 · max numbers 0

| # | Question text | Accepted | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1 — ___ well` | `mother` | "At the upper end, near the hills, is the deepest shaft, which is known as the mother well." |
| 2 | `2 — layer of ___` | `gravel` | "the water-bearing layer, which in most cases is a layer of gravel lying underneath the foothills." |
| 3 | `3 — vertical shafts: for ___ and removing soil` | `ventilation` · `air` | "they also provide ventilation for the workers underground." Removing the soil is already on the label, so the gap is the second purpose. "Access for repairs" is a third purpose, and it isn't the one on the label. |
| 4 | `4 — baked clay ___ (lining the tunnel)` | `hoops` · `hoop` | "the tunnel is lined with oval hoops made of baked clay." On the label, "baked clay" comes *before* the gap, which reverses the order in the lecture. |

### Group 2 — Flow Chart Completion (Q5–9)
**Instructions:** Complete the flow chart below. Write ONE WORD ONLY for each answer.
**Flow chart title:** Building a qanat

| # | Step | Accepted | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | `Look for ___ at the base of the hills` | `vegetation` · `plants` | "The surveyor would look carefully at the base of the hills for vegetation. A line of green plants…" Both words are said, and each refers to the same sign. |
| 6 | `Dig the mother well and monitor it for a full ___` | `year` | "They would monitor the well for a full year, including a dry summer." The flow chart reuses "for a full", so the gap is the unit of time. "summer" is heard right after it but is only part of that year. |
| 7 | `Dig the tunnel, starting from the ___ end` | `outlet` | "they started at the outlet end, out on the plain, and dug uphill." The lecturer first describes what you *might* expect (starting from the mother well), which is the trap. |
| 8 | `Keep the tunnel straight by lining up ___ in two neighbouring shafts` | `lamps` | "hanging lamps in two neighbouring shafts, and lining them up by eye." |
| 9 | `Keep the slope shallow to prevent ___ of the tunnel walls` | `erosion` | "If the water ran too fast, it would cause erosion of the tunnel walls." |

### Group 3 — Sentence Completion (Q10–12)
**Instructions:** Complete the sentences below. Write ONE WORD ONLY for each answer.

| # | Sentence | Accepted | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | `Qanats lose very little water to ___, unlike open canals.` | `evaporation` | "because the water travels underground, very little of it is lost to evaporation." |
| 11 | `Many qanats ran dry after wells with ___ pumps lowered the water table.` | `diesel` | "deep wells with diesel pumps were drilled across the region. They lowered the water table." The gap is the kind of pump, so only an adjective or noun modifier fits. Prediction narrows it before you hear it. |
| 12 | `In 2016, eleven Iranian qanats were added to UNESCO's World ___ list.` | `Heritage` | "added to UNESCO's World Heritage list." The spoken year is "twenty sixteen", so recognising it links this sentence to the right part of the lecture. |
