WARDEN: The main path finishes at the south shore of the lake. You'll see some benches and a little wooden jetty there, and a lot of visitors assume that's where the bird hide is. It isn't. The hide is on the opposite side, on the far, northern shore, so you have to walk right round the lake to reach it. It faces south, which means the light's behind you in the afternoon.

WARDEN: And finally, the beach clean. If you take the path to the right from the first-aid point, it crosses the stream on a small bridge, and then goes on through the dunes to the beach. We don't meet on the beach itself, because it's very hard to find each other in the dunes. The meeting point is the bridge, and we walk down together from there.

WARDEN: OK. Now let me tell you how the week works. Each day has one main task. WARDEN: Tuesdays are for scrub. That means cutting back the bushes that are spreading into the grassland. It's hard work, but it's probably the most useful thing we do. And no tree planting, before anyone asks. This is grassland, and we want it to stay that way.
